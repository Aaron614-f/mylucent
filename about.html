A NOTE FROM THE MAKER

It started as
a hobby.

Around ten years ago I began experimenting with laser cutting just to see what it could do, and I couldn’t put it down. I tried every material and every style I could get my hands on, engraving and cutting, one experiment after another.

Slowly the hobby turned into a craft, and the craft turned into something far more serious. Along the way I worked out my own tricks for getting clean, sharp, lasting results, the kind you only learn by doing it hundreds of times.

FROM THE GALLERY

Today that experience is myLucent.co. I make custom acrylic art, whatever you have in mind:

  • Wall artA design made for your space, in your colours.
  • GiftsSomething personal for a birthday, a wedding, a thank-you or any occasion.
  • Simcha souvenirsA keepsake for your guests, made for your simcha.

If it’s acrylic, I can make it. There’s no catalogue to choose from. You come to me with an idea, even a rough one, and we build it together, step by step, until it’s exactly what you pictured.

I’m waiting to hear from you. Get in touch and I’ll start on your project as soon as I can.
