TEST BEFORE GOING LIVE
  1. In Railway, temporarily use your Stripe TEST keys (sk_test_... and the test webhook secret).
  2. Go to /checkout.html, fill in details, pay with card 4242 4242 4242 4242, any future date, any CVC.
  3. Check: you land back on the home page with "Thank you — your order is confirmed",
     you get the order text, and the customer email arrives.
  4. Switch back to the live keys.

DESIGN PREFERENCE (checkout.html)
  Customers must pick Design 1 (Arches), 2 (Key pattern) or 3 (Shards) before paying,
  with the note "While stocks last. If your chosen design is no longer available, we will contact you to choose the next-best preference."
  The choice shows in your order text ("Pref: ..."), the customer email, and the Stripe payment description.
  Design names live in pricing.js under products -> designs. Images: assets/design-1.svg, -2, -3.

AFTER PAYMENT (Chanukah stand) — only once Stripe confirms the payment:
  1. Admin Inbox: a new unread message from the customer with the full order
     (reference, item, quantity, preferred design, total paid, name, mobile, email).
  2. One short text to you, e.g.
        New paid order
        Rivka Cohen
        0412345678
        Pref: Design 2 (Key pattern)
  3. The customer gets their confirmation email.
  Nothing is sent if they stop before paying.
  When the piece is ready, text "ML-XXXXXXX ready" (reference is in the Inbox message)
  to email the customer that it's ready for pickup.
